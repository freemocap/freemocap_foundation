I am writing on behalf of Gibbet Games regarding our ongoing use of and collaboration with the FreeMoCap project. Gibbet Games is an independent video game studio, and our work includes character animation and motion-capture-based production for projects including *Esoteric Ebb*.

**[Describe how FreeMoCap became part of your production workflow and why it is useful for your work.]**

*Possible language:*
We began using FreeMoCap as part of the character-animation workflow for *Esoteric Ebb*. Our animation process often requires us to capture motion, review it, and quickly record new takes when an animation needs to change. Although we have access to a local marker-based motion-capture studio, using a dedicated studio requires scheduling and rental time, which can make this kind of iteration difficult. FreeMoCap allows us to record in our own workspace using consumer cameras and to work directly with the resulting video and motion data. We have used FreeMoCap-based motion capture throughout the character-animation process for *Esoteric Ebb* and plan to continue using it in future projects.

**[Describe how the motion-capture data are actually used in your animation process.]**

*Possible language:*
The motion-capture recordings are applied to 3D character models, which are then used as a reference for hand-drawn animation. This allows our animators to work from captured human motion while retaining the visual style and flexibility of the final animation.

**[Describe your interaction with the FreeMoCap team and the kinds of animation-specific needs you have discussed.]**

*Possible language:*
Through our use of FreeMoCap, we have been in contact with the development team about the needs of animators and game developers. These discussions have included recording workflows, handling scenes where multiple people may appear in the camera views, and ways to make synchronized video more useful as animation reference. 

**[Describe any tools, workflows, or contributions you are currently developing or would like to contribute back to the community.]**

*Possible language:*
We are also beginning to develop tools around these workflows. One current example is a Maya tool for importing and editing FreeMoCap animation data while keeping the recorded video, camera views, skeleton, and animation synchronized. We intend to continue developing and testing animation-focused workflows like this and, where possible, make them available to the FreeMoCap community.
