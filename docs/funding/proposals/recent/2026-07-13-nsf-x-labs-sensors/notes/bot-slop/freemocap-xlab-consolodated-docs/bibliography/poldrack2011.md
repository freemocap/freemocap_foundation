# Poldrack et al. (2011) — The Cognitive Atlas
**Citation:** Poldrack, R. A., Kittur, A., Kalar, D., et al. (2011). The Cognitive Atlas... *Frontiers in Neuroinformatics* 5, 17. `@poldrack2011` · doi:10.3389/fninf.2011.00017

**What it is:** A knowledge base of **cognitive constructs and the tasks that measure them** — a formal-camp effort to organize cognitive neuroscience's conceptual vocabulary.

**Why we cite it:** Another instance of the formal-ontology camp (with [[turner2012|CogPO]]): describes constructs/tasks, not the calibrated multimodal interaction stream. Reinforces that **none** of the existing scientific ontologies is the operational middle layer ([[the-ontology-question-in-science]], [[the-missing-middle]]).
