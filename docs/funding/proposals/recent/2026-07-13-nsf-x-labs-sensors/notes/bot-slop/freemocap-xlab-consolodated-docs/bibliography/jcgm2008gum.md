# JCGM 100:2008 — Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM)
**Citation:** Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (2008). *GUM (JCGM 100:2008).* BIPM. `@jcgm2008gum`

**What it is:** The international framework for evaluating and expressing **measurement uncertainty** — Type A/B components, the law of propagation, coverage factors, expanded uncertainty.

**Why we cite it:** The methodological backbone of the [[uncertainty-budget]] — how every derived [[ontology-definition|ontological object]] carries propagated uncertainty back to the primary sensor. Turns instrument claims into numeric, checkable milestones ([[metrology-and-traceability]]).
