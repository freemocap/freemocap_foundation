# ISO/IEC 17025:2017 — Competence of Testing and Calibration Laboratories
**Citation:** ISO/IEC (2017). *ISO/IEC 17025:2017.* `@iso17025`

**What it is:** Requires calibration/testing labs to demonstrate competence and participate in **proficiency testing / interlaboratory comparisons** (cf. the CIPM MRA's key comparisons among NMIs).

**Why we cite it:** The template for **round-robin / proficiency comparisons across adopting labs** — the mechanism that lets a capture made in one lab be trusted in another, making distributed science cumulative rather than one-off ([[metrology-and-traceability]], [[the-validation-triangle]], [[tiered-dissemination-and-communal-science]]).
