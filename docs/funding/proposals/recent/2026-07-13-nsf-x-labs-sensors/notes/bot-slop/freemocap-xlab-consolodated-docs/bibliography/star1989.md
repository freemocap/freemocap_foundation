# Star & Griesemer (1989) — Institutional Ecology, 'Translations' and Boundary Objects
**Citation:** Star, S. L., & Griesemer, J. R. (1989). Institutional Ecology, 'Translations' and Boundary Objects. *Social Studies of Science* 19(3), 387–420. `@star1989` · doi:10.1177/030631289019003001

**What it is:** Defines **boundary objects** — artifacts "plastic enough to adapt to local needs, yet robust enough to maintain a common identity across sites" — that let different social worlds coordinate without a shared discipline.

**Why we cite it:** The science-studies name for what a well-designed tool becomes: **build for a [[boundary-objects|hypothetical student]] and get a cross-disciplinary commons**. Skipping "step zero" freezes out experts from *other* disciplines, so designing for the student is the only way a physicist, biologist, and roboticist can all enter — structural value, not outreach.
