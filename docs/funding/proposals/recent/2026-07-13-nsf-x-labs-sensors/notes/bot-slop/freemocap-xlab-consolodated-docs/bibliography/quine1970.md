# Quine & Ullian (1970) — The Web of Belief
**Citation:** Quine, W. V. O., & Ullian, J. S. (1970). *The Web of Belief*. Random House. `@quine1970`

**What it is:** The book-length, accessible development of Quine's confirmation holism — the "web of belief" metaphor and how evidence propagates through it. (Hilary Putnam endorsed this holism.)

**Why we cite it:** The citable source for the **web-of-belief** framing of the shared ontology as a coherent, revisable, evidence-anchored structure. Companion to [[quine1951]] and [[kuhn1962]].
