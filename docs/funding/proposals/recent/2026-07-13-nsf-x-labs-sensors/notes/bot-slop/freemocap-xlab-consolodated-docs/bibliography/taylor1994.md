# Taylor & Kuyatt (1994) — NIST Technical Note 1297
**Citation:** Taylor, B. N., & Kuyatt, C. E. (1994). *Guidelines for Evaluating and Expressing the Uncertainty of NIST Measurement Results (NIST TN 1297).* `@taylor1994` · nist.gov

**What it is:** The **US operationalization of the GUM** — Type A/B uncertainty, the law of propagation, coverage factor *k* (commonly *k*=2 for ~95%), expanded uncertainty.

**Why we cite it:** The concrete recipe for stating milestone uncertainties the way NIST does (e.g. "3D joint-center expanded uncertainty `< X mm`, *k*=2"). The go-to citation when the proposal reports numeric benchmarks with proper uncertainty ([[uncertainty-budget]], [[metrology-and-traceability]]).
