# JCTLM — Joint Committee for Traceability in Laboratory Medicine
**Citation:** JCTLM (2007–). *Database of Certified Reference Materials, Reference Methods, and Reference Measurement Services.* `@jctlm` · jctlm.org

**What it is:** Maintains a **public registry** of certified reference materials/methods that manufacturers pick from to make their assays traceable — and therefore comparable across instruments, labs, and countries.

**Why we cite it:** The **governance-model template** for the org play: a US-anchored, vendor-neutral **reference-and-traceability registry** for agent–environment measurement. This is how a field becomes cumulative and how standard-setting becomes soft power ([[metrology-and-traceability]], [[tiered-dissemination-and-communal-science]]).
