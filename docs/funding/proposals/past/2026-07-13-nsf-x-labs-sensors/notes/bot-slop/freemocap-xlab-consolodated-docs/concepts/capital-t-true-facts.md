# Capital-T True Facts

The **capital-T True Facts** are *what actually is* — the metaphysical desiderata of the [[the-missing-middle|Real World]], whether or not anyone measures them. They sit on the **metaphysics** side of [[the-epistemic-gap]]. Our measurements and [[ontology-definition|ontological models]] live on the **epistemology** side — *what we believe is true*.

The instrument is the **bridge across the gap**: it turns [[eaee|environmental energy]] into a defensible, uncertainty-bounded *belief* about the True Facts. It never reaches them — that reach is "the fundamental reach for the impossibility of true knowledge," which JSM frames as the core principle of human science. What the instrument can do is make the belief **grounded** ([[sensor-as-transducer]], [[metrological-traceability]]) and make explicit *how confident* to be ([[uncertainty-budget]]).

This is why [[complete-empirical-capture]] is aspirational: complete capture would be perfect knowledge of the True Facts, which is the unreachable north star that nonetheless defines directionality.
