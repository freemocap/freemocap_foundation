# Makeig et al. (2009) — Mobile Brain/Body Imaging (MoBI)
**Citation:** Makeig, S., Gramann, K., Jung, T.-P., Sejnowski, T. J., & Poizner, H. (2009). Linking Brain, Mind and Behavior: The Promise of Mobile Brain/Body Imaging. *Int. J. Psychophysiology* 73(2), 95–100. `@makeig2009` · doi:10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2008.11.008

**What it is:** Coins **MoBI** — the synchronous collection of brain activity (EEG), behavior (motion capture, eye tracking), and environmental events to study joint brain/body dynamics during natural cognition in 3D.

**Why we cite it:** The named ~15-year peer-reviewed precedent that **validates the concept** (not crankish) *and* **exposes the gap** — MoBI is a methodology, not a platform; labs assemble it by hand from incompatible parts. The absence of the instrument is the bottleneck ([[mobi]], [[the-missing-middle]]).
