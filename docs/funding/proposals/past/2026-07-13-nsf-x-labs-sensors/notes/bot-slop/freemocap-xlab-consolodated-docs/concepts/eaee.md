# EAEE — Empirically Available Environmental Energy

**EAEE** is the coinage for *the physical energy in the environment that a sensor can, in principle, transduce into a measurement* — light, force, acceleration, sound, heat, electrical and chemical signal. It is the substance at the very bottom of the [[the-rw-to-ontology-pipeline|capture pipeline]]: the [[the-missing-middle|world]] is "suffused with" EAEE, and every [[sensor-as-transducer|sensor is a transducer]] that converts one band of it into a repeatable electronic pattern.

## Why the term matters
It names the physical floor that keeps the whole project on the **instrument** side of [[the-software-exclusion|the software exclusion]]. An ontology whose objects bottom out in transduced EAEE is grounded (see [[symbol-grounding-problem]]); one that doesn't is "just software."

## Relationship to Gibson
EAEE is essentially **[[gibson-ecological-optics|Gibson's ambient energy array]] named from the instrument's side of the transduction**. Gibson's ecological optics says the environment already carries perceptual information in structured ambient energy; EAEE is that same structured energy considered as *a thing an instrument samples*. This is the hinge that makes the capture instrument the *right* instrument for a science of perceptual-motor control: the organism and the instrument are doing the **same category of thing** — sampling EAEE.
