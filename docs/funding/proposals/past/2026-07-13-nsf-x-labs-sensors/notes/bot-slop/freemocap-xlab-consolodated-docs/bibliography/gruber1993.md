# Gruber (1993) — A Translation Approach to Portable Ontology Specifications
**Citation:** Gruber, T. R. (1993). A Translation Approach to Portable Ontology Specifications. *Knowledge Acquisition* 5(2), 199–220. `@gruber1993` · doi:10.1006/knac.1993.1008 · *(verified this session)*

**What it is:** The paper that gave computer science its canonical definition: **"an ontology is an explicit specification of a conceptualization."** Introduces Ontolingua for portable ontology definitions across representation systems.

**Why we cite it:** The definitional anchor for how we use "[[ontology-definition|ontology]]" — the engineering sense (typed model of entities/relations), grounded by the metaphysical sense. Establishes that the word predates and is broader than the Semantic Web's OWL usage, licensing our pragmatic property-graph choice ([[the-ontology-question-in-science]]).
