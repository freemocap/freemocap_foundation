# The Validation Triangle

The three legs that make the distributed instrument **one instrument, not several projects** — each leg validates and extends the others: **precision ↔ portability ↔ biological depth.**

1. **Wing A — the Boston instrumented volume (room).** Maximum precision, manipulable environment (VR/AR/MR), ground-truth reference.
2. **Wing B — the wearable/outdoor rig (reach).** Portable, naturalistic, productizable; its drift-prone estimates are **calibrated *inside* Wing A first** (a [[metrological-traceability|traceability chain]] A→B), then taken outdoors.
3. **The animal-model network (depth).** The invasive neural modalities that can't go on humans — [[comparative-phylogeny-and-ecs|ferret Neuropixels/miniscope, mouse optogenetics, guinea-fowl EMG]] — live here.

What fuses the three into one instrument: the shared [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]], [[metrological-traceability|metrological traceability]] (every site's measurements uncertainty-tagged, commensurable across labs/species/equipment tiers — a mouse rig and the Boston volume differ in *uncertainty, not in kind*), the [[shared-vs-unshared-traits|comparative design]], the [[tiered-dissemination-and-communal-science|tiered dissemination]], and the [[ontology-grounded-qc-copilot|QC copilot]].
