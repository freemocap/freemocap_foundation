# Turner & Laird (2012) — The Cognitive Paradigm Ontology (CogPO)
**Citation:** Turner, J. A., & Laird, A. R. (2012). The Cognitive Paradigm Ontology: Design and Application. *Neuroinformatics* 10(1), 57–66. `@turner2012` · doi:10.1007/s12021-011-9126-x

**What it is:** A **BFO-aligned formal ontology** of experimental conditions (stimulus modality, response, instructions), derived from the BrainMap taxonomy, distributed as `.owl` files.

**Why we cite it:** Exhibit A for the **formal-ontology camp** — academically respected, operationally stalled, describing *experiments* rather than raw multimodal data ([[the-ontology-question-in-science]]). Part of the evidence that formal Semantic-Web ontologies never achieved operational penetration in neuroscience.
