# Bloom (1984) — The 2 Sigma Problem
**Citation:** Bloom, B. S. (1984). The 2 Sigma Problem: The Search for Methods of Group Instruction as Effective as One-to-One Tutoring. *Educational Researcher* 13(6), 4–16. `@bloom1984` · doi:10.3102/0013189X013006004

**What it is:** One-on-one tutoring moves the average student ~**two standard deviations** above classroom instruction — but human tutoring doesn't scale.

**Why we cite it:** The scaling problem AI just changed, and the frame for the PI's **6 semesters** of AI-augmented teaching ([[bloom-2-sigma]], [[conceptual-cleanliness-four-audiences]]). **Keep the hedge** `#verify`: AI-tutoring evidence is early/mixed — cite the effect, don't overclaim "2σ" (see [[_verification-punchlist]]).
