# Beck (2019) — On the Hourglass Model
**Citation:** Beck, M. (2019). On the Hourglass Model. *Communications of the ACM* 62(7), 48–57. `@beck2019` · doi:10.1145/3274770

**What it is:** Names the **"Deployment Scalability Trade-off"**: simplicity and generality at the waist beat rich, featureful designs on real-world adoption.

**Why we cite it:** The citable statement that a **thin, controlled waist** scales where a fat standard ossifies. Directly supports treating [[lab-streaming-layer|LSL/XDF]] as an outside adapter, not the inside timing substrate ([[build-to-spec-vs-shared-protocols]], [[narrow-waist]]).
