**Citation:** Mathis, A., Mamidanna, P., Cury, K. M., Abe, T., Murthy, V. N., Mathis, M. W., & Bethge, M. (2018). DeepLabCut: Markerless Pose Estimation of User-Defined Body Parts with Deep Learning. *Nature Neuroscience* 21(9), 1281–1289. `@mathis2018dlc` · doi:10.1038/s41593-018-0209-y · *(verified this session)*
