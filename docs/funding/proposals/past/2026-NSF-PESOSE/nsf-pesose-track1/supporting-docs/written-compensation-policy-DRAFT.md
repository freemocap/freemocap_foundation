# FreeMoCap Foundation — Written Compensation Policy (DRAFT — for board approval)

*Adopted by the Board of Directors on ____________, 2026 (Resolution No. ______)*

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the FreeMoCap Foundation's written compensation
policies for its personnel, consistent with the requirement that salary rates
for newly employed personnel supported on grants from U.S. federal agencies be
consistent with the established, written policies of the employing
organization.

## 2. Compensation Structure

Compensation for Foundation personnel is set by the Board of Directors based
on the functional responsibilities of each position, with reference to
prevailing market rates for corresponding occupations (Bureau of Labor
Statistics Standard Occupational Classification categories) in the
geographic area where the work is performed. Compensation is paid through
formal W-2 employment.

## 3. Initial Compensation Schedule

The Board adopts the following initial annual base salaries, effective upon
each individual's transition to W-2 employment:

| Role | Functional title (SOC classification) | Annual base salary |
|---|---|---|
| President / Principal Investigator | Computer and Information Research Scientist (SOC 15-1221) | $100,000 |
| Chief Scientific Officer / co-Principal Investigator | Software Developer (SOC 15-1252) | $75,000 |
| Operations Lead | Network and Computer Systems Administrator (SOC 15-1244) | $50,000 |

## 4. Escalation and Review

The Board will review compensation annually and may adjust salaries for
merit, escalation, or changes in responsibility. Any adjustment beyond 10%
in a single year requires a recorded board vote.

## 5. Benefits

Benefits-eligible personnel receive: employer contributions toward health
insurance coverage, a retirement plan, and statutory payroll contributions,
as enacted in the Foundation's benefits plan. The fringe benefit rate
applied to sponsored awards reflects the actual cost of this benefits
structure.

## 6. Amendment

This policy may be amended by a majority vote of the Board of Directors.
