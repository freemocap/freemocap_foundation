# Sensor-Grounded Ontology

> *The candidate reframe title for the whole proposal: **"A convergent science of perceptual-motor control through sensor-grounded ontologies."***

This is the **load-bearing thread** for the solicitation's instrumentation requirement. It fixes the risk that the [[composition-over-inheritance|software-philosophy]] material reads as middleware, by naming the physical anchor that makes the enterprise **hard-science instrumentation**: the ontology is *sensor-grounded*.

## The stack, bracketed by two empirical anchors

| Layer | What it is | Why it's instrumentation |
|---|---|---|
| **Bottom — grounding** | a [[sensor-as-transducer|sensor array]] transduces [[eaee|EAEE]] into repeatable electronic patterns | transduction + [[calibration]] + [[metrological-traceability]] to SI, with [[uncertainty-budget|uncertainty]] at every step |
| **Middle — complexity mgmt** | truth-preserving pipelines construct grounded [[ontology-definition|ontological objects]] ([[composition-over-inheritance|entities/traits/relations]]) | a *calibrated measurement transformation*, [[symbol-grounding-problem|sensor-grounded]] (Harnad/Brooks) — not "just software" |
| **Top — human interface** | the ontology presented to the scientist under managed cognitive load | the human is the final reader in the measurement chain; the readout has always been part of the instrument |

Environmental energy at the bottom, a human perceiver at the top. The software content is the **necessary connective tissue in the middle** — elevated to a first-order concern precisely because *managing the complexity of grounded, multimodal, uncertainty-bearing measurement is itself a problem in measurement science.*

## The one-sentence defense against [[the-software-exclusion]] `#reframe`
> We do not build software that happens to touch sensors; we build **a measurement instrument whose readings are [[ontology-definition|ontological objects]], constructed by truth-preserving pipelines from calibrated, [[metrological-traceability|traceable]] transduction of environmental energy.**

## Why it's the right instrument for *perceptual-motor* science
Nervous systems are, at their sensory surface, exactly this — [[sensor-as-transducer|arrays of transducers]] of environmental energy. So a science of how animals control action on sensed information is, at core, an instrumentation problem: measure the same [[eaee|EAEE]] the animal samples, *and* the animal's sampling of it (gaze, pose, force), simultaneously and commensurably. The evidence base already exists — [[personnel|Matthis 2018/2022]] fused mobile eye tracking + IMU mocap outdoors and built retinal optic flow as a *new ontological object* via a calibrated projective-geometry construction. The **convergence** across [[comparative-phylogeny-and-ecs|species, scales, and modalities]] — each collaboration bolting a new transducer class (neural, EMG, force) onto the same grounded ontology — *is* the "convergent science."

## The two ends of one measurement chain `#design-lesson`
Complexity management (facing the sensors) and **cognitive-load management** (facing the scientist, per [[conceptual-cleanliness-four-audiences|Sweller's Cognitive Load Theory]]) are the *same* first-order problem attacked at two ends. Both are why the tool becomes "an instrument people actually use" rather than [[opensim|another thing too complicated for anyone]] — usability is the adoption mechanism.
