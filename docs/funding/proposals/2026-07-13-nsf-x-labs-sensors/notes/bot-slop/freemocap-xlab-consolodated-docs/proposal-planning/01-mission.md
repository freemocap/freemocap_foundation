# §1 Mission — planning

> [!quote] What the solicitation asks (§10.1)
> "Describe one or more **novel platform technologies** that the proposed NSF X-Lab is uniquely suited to advance. Successful teams will have a **clear vision statement** of how their work will result in an end-state that **reshapes an entire field** of scientific inquiry or a sector of technology, and **how their Mission is currently unmet** by existing organizational structures and funding mechanisms."

**Earns:** Mission Clarity & Outcomes — §11.2a ned) + §11.2c (aligned with the §3.0 objectives). Seeds the org-structure case too. · **Budget:** ~1.0 pg.

## The job of this section
Land the [[the-thesis-instrument-not-software|spine]] in one tight arc: **new observable → instrument → field reshaped → why unmet**. A reviewer should finish this page able to picture the before/after and know this needs an X-Lab.

## Beats to hit (outline)
1. **Hook — the measurement-revolution register.** Open in Topic-2's own frame (measurement revolutions precede scientific revolutions); the un-instrumented frontier = the whole behaving agent in its natural environment. [[naturalistic-neuroscience]]
2. **The new observable.** Name it as a *measurable*, not a tool: the calibrated, synchronized, semantically-unified record of the agent–environment interaction → [[complete-empirical-capture]]. This is question #1 of [[the-three-load-bearing-questions]].
3. **The instrument (one sentence, measurement-first).** An [[sensor-as-transducer|array of transducers]] unified by a [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]] + [[metrology-and-traceability|traceable pipelines]] → make it concrete immediately with the tangible deliverable: the two-wing [[the-physical-instrument-boston-flagship-and-network|Boston flagship + animal-model network]].
4. **The field reshaped.** A convergent science of perceptual-motor control, unlocked by making interaction a first-class cumulative measurable ([[the-missing-middle]]). *Decision: name it as a new field vs. describe it (see open decisions).*
5. **Both X-Labs objectives, explicitly.** The instrument = the *platform technology*; the FreeMoCap Foundation + tiered network + governance = the *new organizational research structure*.
6. **The "unmet by existing structures & funding" clause.** Compress [[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]] to 1–2 sentences: normal funding structurally yields disposable single-modality prototypes; this needs full-time, autonomous, IP-owning, sustained engineering.

## Say explicitly `#say`
The new observable; instrument-not-software; the concrete flagship as the Mission object; one crisp "unmet" clause; both objectives named.

## Allude only (a clause) `#allude`
[[the-epistemic-gap|Epistemic-gap]] philosophy; [[dimensional-domains]]; the deep academic-incentive critique; the [[architectural-paradigm-operational-semantic-layer|Palantir/Anduril]] lineage.
