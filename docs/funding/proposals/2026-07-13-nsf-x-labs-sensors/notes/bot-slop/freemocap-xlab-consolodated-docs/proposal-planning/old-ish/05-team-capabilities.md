# §5 Team Capabilities Statement — planning

> [!quote] What the solicitation asks (§10.1)
> "Describe how the Senior/Key Personnel bring together **complementary expertise in strategic leadership, technical expertise, and/or entrepreneurship**... how, collectively, the team demonstrates the **experience, adaptability, and collaborative approach** necessary to execute the Mission. **Briefly describe the team's intended governance structure and autonomy during Phase 0 and Phase 1.** If applicable, include an overview of **networks, partnerships, and capital resources**."

**Earns:** Team Qualifications & Structure — §11.1b (Phase-1 governance appropriate, leverages novel partnerships, provides autonomy/flexibility). With §4 this is **half the score**. · **Budget:** ~1.75 pg.

## The job of this section
Show the team functions as one adaptable unit, and — decisively — **nail governance + autonomy**. The [[autonomy-factor-test|Autonomy Factor Test]] is the central Phase-1 bar; this section is where it's answered.

## Beats to hit (outline)
1. **Collective capability.** Complementary strategic leadership + technical depth + entrepreneurship; **adaptability** evidenced concretely — FreeMoCap's repeated ground-up refactors as proof of adaptive execution ([[path-dependence]]), and shipping an adopted open tool.
2. **The network as deployable capital.** The ongoing animal-model labs ([[personnel]]); the FreeMoCap community (~15k users / 150+ countries / ~3.5k Discord `#verify`); the planned **annual convening** as the federation/trading-zone mechanism.
3. **Governance (intended, Phase 0 → Phase 1).** [[bdfl-governance|BDfaSPoT]]: President → President + Board → President + Board (C-suite) + Community DAO; public RFC process (PEP-style); Year-2 Developer Fund + Community Grants. Frame as the deliberate [[conways-law|Inverse Conway Maneuver]] that produces the desired open, refactorable architecture.
4. **Autonomy (answer the factor test explicitly).** The FreeMoCap Foundation is an **institutionally independent 501c3** — "already autonomous": full internal control of IP, funding allocation, hiring, research direction; independent board; decisions in days not weeks. [[autonomy-factor-test]]
5. **Partnerships across sectors.** Industry, government, academia, philanthropy — the cross-sector leverage §11.1b explicitly rewards.

## Say explicitly `#say`
Governance transition plan; the **autonomy factor test answered concretely** (IP/funding/hiring control, independent board); the collaboration network + annual convening as capital; cross-sector partnerships.

## Allude only (a clause) `#allude`
The deep FOSS-governance theory (Torvalds/Blender, Eghbal quadrants) → one line; the full academic-incentive critique ([[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]]) → compress.

## Landmines
- Autonomy claims must be **concrete and checkable** (name the IP/funding/hiring control), not aspirational hand-waving — the factor test is a real gate.
- Don't let governance read as bureaucracy; frame it as *what enables speed and independence*.
- This section + §4 are half the score — do not treat as an afterthought.

## Open decisions (JSM)
- **Board composition** and the President-majority-control timeline (≥51% to year 5?). `#verify`
- Which **partnerships / capital resources** to name (facility, philanthropic, industry).
- Facility **buy vs. lease** insofar as it touches capital resources.
