# The NSF X-Labs Opportunity & Fit

The funding target, its mechanics, and why the vision fits it unusually well. Source of truth: the official `.md` ports of the OTASO + Topic-2 solicitations — verify content against them, not memory.

## What X-Labs is and wants
Run by NSF's **Directorate for Technology, Innovation and Partnerships (TIP)** under the **Other Transactions Agreement Solutions Offering (OTASO)**. Funds **full-time, autonomous, milestone-funded R&D teams** to build "novel platform technologies capable of unlocking entirely new sectors, including new fields of scientific inquiry" — explicitly for work "traditional university and industry labs cannot easily address."

**Structure:** Phase 0 (~9–12 mo, ≤ **$1.5M**, prep + milestone/budget refinement) → Phase 1 Go/No-Go → Phase 1 (~24–36 mo, ≤ **$50M/yr**) → Phase 2 (variable) → possible Phase 3. Milestone-based OT contracts; Phase-1 budgets submitted at **three tiers** (essential/mid/comprehensive).

**Dual strategic objectives (§3.0):** *Build Sector-Defining Platforms* ("beyond traditional outputs like publications and datasets") + *Develop New Organizational Research Structures* ("challenges that lack sufficient incentives within traditional academic and industry labs"). §7.0 names exemplar platform techs: **VLSI, the Internet, PCR, brain-computing interfaces, next-gen sequencing, AlphaFold, LEDs.**

## Topic 2 — Scientific Instrumentation for Sensing and Imaging
Opens with *"Every revolution in science has been preceded by a revolution in what we can measure"* (telescope → MRI). In-scope examples include *"whole-brain activity at cellular resolution across long timescales"* and — tellingly — *"instruments intentionally engineered for next-generation AI training pipelines"* (our [[the-rw-to-ontology-pipeline|bio↔artificial]] hook).

## The exclusions — the knife to avoid `#framing-hazard`
See [[the-software-exclusion]]. The sharpest: *"computational or software solutions without practical integration into an instrumentation system."* Also: incremental SOTA; narrow/not-deployable; fundamental research w/o platform application; already-commercializable; **"platform technologies where the sole challenges are non-technical."**

## The proposal format (verify against PDFs)
**8 single-sided pages max** (excl. COI), single-spaced, 12-pt, 1" margins, US-letter, PDF, no cover letter. Five content areas: **Mission · Technology Landscape · Outcomes · Senior/Key Personnel Qualifications · Team Capabilities Statement** (+ a separate ~5000-char summary). Deadline **2026-07-13, 5pm ET**; orals **Aug 17–21, 2026**; Phase 0 begins **Nov 2026**.

## The selection criteria (weighted ~equally)
1. **Team Qualifications and Structure** — complementary Senior/Key expertise + demonstrated record; a Phase-1 governance structure with the [[autonomy-factor-test|autonomy/flexibility]] to leverage novel partnerships.
2. **Mission Clarity and Outcomes** — ambitious/technically-sound/well-defined Mission; landscape justifies a *dedicated full-time team*; aligned with the strategic objectives.

The equal weighting means the [[personnel|team/governance]] half is **not** an afterthought — it is half the score. The [[autonomy-factor-test|Autonomy Factor Test]] is the central Phase-1 bar.

## Why the fit is unusually good
- The **"platform technology that unlocks new fields"** mandate matches the pitch's shape directly.
- The **new-measurement-modality register** is available: [[complete-empirical-capture]] is a measurement-revolution argument, not a software one — exactly Topic-2's register.
- The **cross-scale / cross-domain / multi-timescale** framing is native to both the vision and the solicitation's language ([[dimensional-domains]]).
- The **"reshape an entire field"** bar is satisfiable: behavioral/motor/embodied-cognitive neuroscience is bottlenecked by [[the-missing-middle|fragmented measurement]].
