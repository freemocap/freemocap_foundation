# Dimensional Domains

A **dimensional domain** is *any axis along which the agent–environment interaction can vary and still be described by the same [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]].* The claim that [[complete-empirical-capture|capture]] is **scale-free** — the same ontology for fruit-fly mocap as for untethered outdoor human exploration — is the claim that the instrument spans these domains. From JSM's outline, the domains are:

- **Species** — data models mapped to the [[comparative-phylogeny-and-ecs|phylogenetic tree]]: all vertebrates have a skull, all tetrapods have fore/hind limbs. Aligns animal research to human wellbeing.
- **Scale** — spatial alignment across *C. elegans* → fruit flies → mice → monkeys → humans → elephants.
- **Time** — high-density short-timescale measurement (high-speed video) aligned with longitudinal lifespan tracking (a cage across full lifespan).
- **Numerosity** — single/countable animals in a VR/AR lab **and** swarms observed from drones.
- **Complexity** — coarse animal segmentation from satellite **and** precise full-body kinematics.

The point for the proposal: this turns "we study lots of things" into a **principled generality claim** — one calibrated ontology, commensurable across every axis because every measurement is [[metrological-traceability|traceable]] and [[uncertainty-budget|uncertainty-tagged]].
