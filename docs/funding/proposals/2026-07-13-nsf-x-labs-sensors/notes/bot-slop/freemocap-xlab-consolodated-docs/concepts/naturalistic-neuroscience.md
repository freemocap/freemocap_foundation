# Naturalistic Neuroscience (the wind at the back)

Two simultaneous shifts make *now* the right moment, and both should be named:

1. **Markerless capture has crossed the threshold** — [[markerless-pose-estimation|deep-learning pose estimation]] moved kinematics out of the marker-suit/calibrated-lab regime; cheap cameras can feed the capture layer. *The supply-side enabler.*
2. **"Naturalistic" / "real-world" neuroscience is the field's stated frontier** — the move away from the chin-rest, single-modality, static paradigm toward embodied, interacting agents. This is exactly what [[mobi|MoBI]] and the Topic-2 language both point at.

> The vision is the platform this frontier needs and lacks. The un-instrumented frontier = **the whole behaving agent in its natural environment.**

This is also the scientific home of the PI's published work ([[personnel|Matthis 2018/2022]]): gaze + full-body kinematics recorded *outdoors on natural rough terrain* — the agent–environment-interaction instrument in embryo.
