# Bloom's 2-Sigma Problem (education at scale)

**Bloom (1984):** one-on-one tutoring moves the average student ~**two standard deviations** above classroom instruction (better than 98% of conventionally-taught peers) — but human tutoring **doesn't scale.** LLM tutors are the first plausible scalable approximation: a 2024 GPT-4 physics-tutoring RCT found students learned "more than twice as much" (effect sizes ~0.73–1.3σ); adaptive-learning meta-analyses sit around g≈0.70 — short of 2σ but far above typical interventions, and it scales to anyone with a phone.

## The PI already ran this experiment `#say`
[[personnel|JSM]] built and iterated an AI teaching tool across ~6 semesters of *Neural Control of Real-World Human Movement* (a Discord bot serving structured, lecture-tied prompts). Method: lecture at **conference level** (not dumbed-down-for-a-test); tell students to let it wash over them and note only concepts that *tug*; then converse with the bot about those; spend class time teaching *how to converse productively with an AI*. Result: no lowered ceiling, per-student personalization at scale — a lived 2-sigma approximation and a strong "PI is uniquely suited" point.
