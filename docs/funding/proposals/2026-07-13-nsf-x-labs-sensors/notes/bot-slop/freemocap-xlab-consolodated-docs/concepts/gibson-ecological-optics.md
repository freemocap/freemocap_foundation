# Gibson's Ecological Optics

**J.J. Gibson's ecological approach to perception:** the environment provides ecological information in the form of **ambient energy arrays** (the "ambient optic array" for vision), whose invariant structure directly specifies **affordances** — what surfaces are walk-on-able, graspable, sit-on-able (*The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception*, 1979).

This gives the project a **principled, citable theoretical spine** tying hardware directly to the science of perceptual-motor control:

> **[[eaee|EAEE]] is essentially Gibson's ambient energy array, named from the instrument's side of the transduction.** The instrument samples the same structured environmental energy the animal's perceptual systems evolved to pick up.

So the instrument is the *right* instrument for this science because organism and instrument do the **same category of thing** — sample EAEE (see [[sensor-as-transducer]]). The worked example is **[[personnel|Matthis 2022]]**: modeling the eye as a spherical-projection [[functional-equivalence-optical-sensor|optical sensor]] to reconstruct retinal optic flow, then extracting invariants (curl, divergence) — Gibsonian information made a measurement.
