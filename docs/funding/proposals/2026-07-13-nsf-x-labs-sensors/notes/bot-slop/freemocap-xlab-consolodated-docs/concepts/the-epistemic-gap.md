# The Epistemic Gap

The divide between **metaphysics** (*what IS* — the [[capital-t-true-facts]] of the [[the-missing-middle|Real World]]) and **epistemology** (*what we believe is true* — our measurements and [[ontology-definition|ontological models]]). It is the philosophical spine of the [[the-rw-to-ontology-pipeline|hand-drawn sketch]]: the pipeline runs left-to-right and crosses this gap in the middle.

- **Left of the line:** the Real World, suffused with [[eaee|EAEE]], whether or not we measure it.
- **The crossing:** [[sensor-as-transducer|sensor transduction]] → measurement estimate → [[hydration|hydrated]] ontological model.
- **Right of the line:** scientific theory and experimentation.

**The instrument is the bridge.** Everything the proposal builds lives on the crossing — turning environmental energy into an uncertainty-bounded belief about the world. A standardized acknowledgement of this gap, *and the yearning to bridge it anyway*, is what JSM calls the core principle of research.
