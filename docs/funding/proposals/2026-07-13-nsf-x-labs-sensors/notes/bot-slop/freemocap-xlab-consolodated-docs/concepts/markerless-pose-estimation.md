# Markerless Pose Estimation (the supply-side enabler)

Deep-learning pose estimation — **DeepLabCut** (Mathis & Mathis), **SLEAP**, **OpenPose**, **AniPose**, **DANNCE**, and the **[[freemocap|FreeMoCap]]** lineage — has moved kinematics **out of the marker-suit, calibrated-lab regime**, validated against gold standards like **XROMM** (X-ray). Cheap commodity cameras can now feed the capture layer. This is one of the [[naturalistic-neuroscience|two enabling shifts]] — *the supply-side enabler.*

## The competitive read (don't compete here)
Every commercial markerless system ([[competitive-terrain|Theia, Move.ai]]) is **single-modality — kinematics only.** They extract a skeleton from video and sell it into a vertical (sports, clinical, animation). None fuses kinematics with neural, gaze, force, and physiology into a unified calibrated record. `#reframe` So the crowded capture market is **evidence for** the integration-as-instrument thesis, not a threat to it — but the edge is **integration, not capture accuracy**, where well-capitalized teams will win. See [[competitive-terrain]].
