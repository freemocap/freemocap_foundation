# Say vs. Allude (the 8-page discipline)

The rule of thumb for what earns space in 8 pages: the instrument, the measurements, and the numbers get stated; the deep software/philosophy gets a clause. It's about **focus and scoring**, not defending against [[the-software-exclusion]]. Every note in this vault is tagged `#say` or `#allude` where a discipline call applies.

> **Framing rule of thumb:** lead every subsection with the *measurement / instrument* noun; let the software/organization ideas enter only as *why the instrument scales and lasts.*

**Say explicitly `#say`** (these earn the score / clear the exclusion):
- instrument = array of [[sensor-as-transducer|transducers]]; [[calibration]], [[metrological-traceability|traceability]], [[uncertainty-budget|uncertainty]].
- the [[competitive-terrain|landscape gap]] + the quantitative landscape figure.
- measurable 5–7-yr targets + benchmarks.
- one crisp sentence on why it needs a full-time [[autonomy-factor-test|X-Lab]].
- the concrete [[the-physical-instrument-boston-flagship-and-network|two-wing flagship]], named animal collaborations, the [[next-generation-eye-trackers|eye-tracker build]].

**Allude — a clause, not a paragraph `#allude`** (depth lives in the oral round):
- [[composition-over-inheritance|composition/override]], [[worse-is-better]], [[narrow-waist]] → "designed for composability and refactorability," no lecture.
- [[why-this-cant-be-academic-science|academic-incentive critique]] → compress into the "unmet by existing structures" clause.
- [[obo-foundry|OBO]] / Internet precedents → one-line analogies max.
- [[the-epistemic-gap|epistemic-gap philosophy]], [[kuhn-paradigm-shift|Kuhn]] → single framing sentence.

**Do not:** anything that reads as enterprise/middleware software; jargon dumps; over-claiming beyond benchmarks; explaining the philosophy at the cost of the physics.
