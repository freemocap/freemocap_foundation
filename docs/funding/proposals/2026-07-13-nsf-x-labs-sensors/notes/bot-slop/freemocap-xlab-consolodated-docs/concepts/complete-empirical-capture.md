# Complete Empirical Capture (of the agent–environment interaction)

The name for the **new measurement object** the X-Lab produces: *a calibrated, synchronized, semantically-unified record of the whole behaving agent acting in its environment* — kinematics, gaze, neural activity, force, and physiology, all at once, on a common spatial and temporal frame.

## It is an aspirational guiding principle, not a literal claim
From JSM's own memo: "complete empirical capture means we are recording every single possible thing we can record about a given slice of continuous time. It is a **necessarily aspirational goal** — a guiding principle more than a desired outcome. It's a way to define directionality in decision-making." The north star is *perfect knowledge of the [[capital-t-true-facts]] of the universe* — unachievable, but it orients every design choice and every act of measurement across [[the-epistemic-gap]].

## Why it is the whole ballgame
The proposal's single most important reframing: this is **a new scientific observable that becomes knowable that wasn't before** — not a better tool for existing science. The observable is the *unified interaction record itself*; the instrument is what makes it measurable at all. This is what clears [[the-software-exclusion]] and answers question #1 of [[the-three-load-bearing-questions]].

Underpinning it is JSM's principle of **the sanctity of the data** — the raw information flowing off the sensors is our closest connection to empirical reality, so pipelines are built to preserve it.
