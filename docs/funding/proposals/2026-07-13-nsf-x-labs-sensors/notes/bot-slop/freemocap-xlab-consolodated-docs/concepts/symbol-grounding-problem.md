# The Symbol Grounding Problem (the intellectual weapon)

The move that turns the software worry inside out. **Harnad's Symbol Grounding Problem (1990):** symbols manipulated purely by their shapes are meaningless — "how can the meanings of the meaningless symbol tokens be grounded in anything but other meaningless symbols?" Harnad's answer is bottom-up: ground symbols in **non-symbolic sensory and categorical features.** **Brooks's Physical Grounding Hypothesis (1990–91)** makes the same move for robotics: intelligence must be grounded in the physical world through sensors and action — *"the world is its own best model."*

## The exact payoff `#reframe`
> An **ungrounded** ontology *is* just software — free-floating symbols, the thing [[the-software-exclusion|the exclusion]] distrusts. A **[[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded]]** ontology is the opposite: every object is anchored, through a traceable [[metrological-traceability|transduction chain]], to a physical measurement of environmental energy. **The grounding is what converts the software concern into an instrumentation strength.**

Our answer to "isn't this just a data model?" is Harnad's and Brooks's answer: **no — because it bottoms out in transduced physical reality, not in other symbols.** The [[composition-over-inheritance|composition/override machinery]] is the legitimate *upper structure*; the sensor grounding is the *base* that makes it a measurement instrument. State both, in that order.

This same grounding logic reappears in AI: [[conceptual-cleanliness-four-audiences|ontology-grounded LLMs hallucinate far less]] — grounded symbols are reliable, floating ones aren't.
