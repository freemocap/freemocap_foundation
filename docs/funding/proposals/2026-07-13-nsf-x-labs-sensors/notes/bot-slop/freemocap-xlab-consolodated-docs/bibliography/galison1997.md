# Galison (1997) — Image and Logic (trading zones)
**Citation:** Galison, P. (1997). *Image and Logic: A Material Culture of Microphysics.* University of Chicago Press. `@galison1997`

**What it is:** Introduces **trading zones** — interactional spaces where different expert cultures build a shared "pidgin" to collaborate despite lacking a common discipline.

**Why we cite it:** The companion to [[star1989|boundary objects]] for the cross-disciplinary story: the **annual congress** and shared tooling are the trading-zone/federation mechanism that keeps a distributed instrument coherent ([[the-physical-instrument-boston-flagship-and-network]], [[conceptual-cleanliness-four-audiences]]).
