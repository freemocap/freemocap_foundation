# Harnad (1990) — The Symbol Grounding Problem
**Citation:** Harnad, S. (1990). The Symbol Grounding Problem. *Physica D* 42(1–3), 335–346. `@harnad1990` · doi:10.1016/0167-2789(90)90087-6

**What it is:** Poses the foundational problem: symbols manipulated purely by their shapes are meaningless — "how can the meanings of the meaningless symbol tokens be grounded in anything but other meaningless symbols?" Harnad's answer is bottom-up grounding in **non-symbolic sensory and categorical features.**

**Why we cite it:** The **intellectual weapon** that turns the software worry inside out. An ungrounded ontology *is* just software; a [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded]] one bottoms out in transduced physical reality — instrumentation, not symbol-shuffling. Directly answers [[the-software-exclusion]] and reappears in the AI-navigability argument ([[conceptual-cleanliness-four-audiences]]: grounded LLMs hallucinate less). Pair with [[brooks1991]].
