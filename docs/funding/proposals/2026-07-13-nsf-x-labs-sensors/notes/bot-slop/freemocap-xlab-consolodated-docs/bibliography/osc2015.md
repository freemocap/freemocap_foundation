# Open Science Collaboration (2015) — Estimating the Reproducibility of Psychological Science
**Citation:** Open Science Collaboration (Nosek et al.) (2015). Estimating the Reproducibility of Psychological Science. *Science* 349(6251), aac4716. `@osc2015` · doi:10.1126/science.aac4716

**What it is:** The large-scale replication study documenting how science rewards "positive, novel, tidy results" — being *first*, not *useful* or *durable*.

**Why we cite it:** Evidence for **novelty bias** ([[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]] C1): the incentive system literally cannot see the value of integrative, durable infrastructure. The "isn't that just DeepLabCut?" reflex ([[mathis2018dlc]]) is this bias in action.
