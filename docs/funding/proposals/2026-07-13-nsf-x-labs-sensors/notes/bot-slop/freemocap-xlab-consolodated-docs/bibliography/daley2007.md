# Daley, Felix & Biewener (2007) — Proximo-Distal Gradient in Control
**Citation:** Daley, M. A., Felix, G., & Biewener, A. A. (2007). Running stability is enhanced by a proximo-distal gradient in joint neuromechanical control. *Journal of Experimental Biology* 210(3), 383–394. `@daley2007` · [PMC2413410](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2413410/) · *(web-verified this session; **not yet in `FMCF-NSF-XLABS.bib`**)*

**What it is:** In guinea fowl, distal joints (ankle, TMP) act as load-sensitive dampers/springs while proximal joints deliver more stereotyped work — a **proximo-distal gradient**: distal limb control is more strongly shaped by intrinsic mechanics and proprioceptive feedback.

**Why we cite it:** The mechanistic payload of the guinea-fowl node ([[comparative-phylogeny-and-ecs]]) — the kind of **musculoskeletal control** insight the model organism yields, and a concrete example of the biomechanics thread the instrument is built to capture across species.
