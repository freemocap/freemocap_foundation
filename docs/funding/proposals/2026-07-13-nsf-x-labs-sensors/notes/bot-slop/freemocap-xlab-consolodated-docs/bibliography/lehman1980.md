# Lehman (1980) — Laws of Software Evolution
**Citation:** Lehman, M. M. (1980). Programs, Life Cycles, and Laws of Software Evolution. *Proc. IEEE* 68(9), 1060–1076. `@lehman1980` · doi:10.1109/PROC.1980.11805

**What it is:** Empirical laws of real-world ("E-type") software: **continuing change**, **increasing complexity** ("unless work is done to reduce it"), and **declining quality** unless rigorously maintained.

**Why we cite it:** Makes "software rot is a **law**, not a failing" citable ([[path-dependence]] C3). "Rome built on Rome" is Lehman's Increasing Complexity; academia supplies the degrading forces and none of the paydown ([[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]]).
