# Smith et al. (2007) — The OBO Foundry
**Citation:** Smith, B., Ashburner, M., Rosse, C., et al. (2007). The OBO Foundry: Coordinated Evolution of Ontologies to Support Biomedical Data Integration. *Nature Biotechnology* 25(11), 1251–1255. `@smith2007` · doi:10.1038/nbt1346

**What it is:** Establishes the **OBO Foundry** — a governed federation of ~hundreds of interoperating bio-ontologies with shared design principles (openness, orthogonality, a common relation set, the BFO upper ontology).

**Why we cite it:** The **organizational precedent** for the "New Organizational Research Structure" objective — a sustained, federated, governed consortium stewarding precompetitive infrastructure no single lab/company would produce ([[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]], [[obo-foundry]]). Its ~25-year horizon is the argument for the X-Labs funding shape. Also shows biology models by **composition** of orthogonal terms, not one inheritance tree ([[composition-over-inheritance]]).
