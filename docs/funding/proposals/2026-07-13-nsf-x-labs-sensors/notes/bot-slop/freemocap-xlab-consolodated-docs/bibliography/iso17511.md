# ISO 17511:2020 — Metrological Traceability for In-Vitro Diagnostics
**Citation:** ISO (2020). *ISO 17511:2020 — In vitro diagnostic medical devices: Requirements for establishing metrological traceability.* `@iso17511`

**What it is:** Requires every clinical diagnostic result to be **traceable through a calibration hierarchy** to the highest-order reference, with a fit-for-purpose maximum allowable uncertainty.

**Why we cite it:** The **closest precedent to the vision**: substitute "sensor-grounded capture pipelines" for "assays" and "reference motions" for "reference materials" and you have the governance model — be the [[jctlm|JCTLM]] of agent–environment measurement. Central to the "become the reference-and-traceability body" org play ([[metrology-and-traceability]]).
