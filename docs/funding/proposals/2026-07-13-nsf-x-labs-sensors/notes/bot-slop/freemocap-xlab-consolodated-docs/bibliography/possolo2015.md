# Possolo (2015) — NIST Technical Note 1900
**Citation:** Possolo, A. (2015). *Simple Guide for Evaluating and Expressing the Uncertainty of NIST Measurement Results (NIST TN 1900).* `@possolo2015` · nist.gov

**What it is:** A more accessible, worked companion to [[taylor1994|TN 1297]] for evaluating and expressing measurement uncertainty.

**Why we cite it:** Practical support for building [[uncertainty-budget|uncertainty budgets]] on the instrument's pipelines; useful when spelling out a concrete milestone's uncertainty computation.
