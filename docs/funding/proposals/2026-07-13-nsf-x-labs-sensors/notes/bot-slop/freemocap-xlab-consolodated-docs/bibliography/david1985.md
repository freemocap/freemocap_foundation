# David (1985) — Clio and the Economics of QWERTY
**Citation:** David, P. A. (1985). Clio and the Economics of QWERTY. *American Economic Review* 75(2), 332–337. `@david1985`

**What it is:** The canonical **path-dependence / lock-in** paper: QWERTY, chosen for 1870s mechanical constraints, locked in for 150 years despite better alternatives — small early events have disproportionate, non-ergodic long-term effects.

**Why we cite it:** The formal backing for JSM's sharpest claim — *step-zero decisions, made at the point of least knowledge, define the maximum complexity a project can ever handle* ([[path-dependence]] C4). Pair with [[arthur1989]].
