# The PI's Published Corpus — the "laser-skeleton" gaze/gait lineage

An overview of the 12 pre-existing bib entries (already in `FMCF-NSF-XLABS.bib`) — the PI's own research arc, which is the **evidence base** that the agent–environment-capture instrument is real and the PI is uniquely suited. These are cited by `@citekey` directly.

**The core two (the paradigm cases):**
- **`@matthis2018`** — Matthis, Yates & Hayhoe (2018), *Current Biology*. Mobile eye tracker + IMU full-body mocap **outdoors on natural terrain** → an [[sensor-grounded-ontology|instrument array]] in embryo; walkers tune gaze to terrain with a constant look-ahead.
- **`@matthis2022`** — Matthis, Muller, Bonnen & Hayhoe (2022), *PLOS Comp Biol*. A textbook **[[metrology-and-traceability|truth-preserving pipeline]]**: from gaze+kinematics, a calibrated projective-geometry construction builds a *new ontological object* — retinal optic flow — via curl/divergence. The paradigm case for the whole proposal.

**The gait-control lineage (Matthis & Fajen / Barton):** `@matthis2013`, `@matthis2014`, `@matthis2015`, `@matthis2017`, `@barton2017` — the biomechanics-shapes-vision-use program (visual control of foot placement over complex terrain). Incorporates a custom Projected Groundplane Interactively Tied to full-body (vicon) motion capture., which is a way to emphasize how well suited the PI-JSM is to build on the proposed work. 

**The retinal-motion follow-ups (Muller et al.):** `@muller2022`, `@muller2023`, `@muller2024` — retinal motion statistics + foothold selection integrating eye tracking, mocap, and photogrammetry.

**The theory & binocular anchors:** `@warren2006` (behavioral dynamics — the agent–environment coupled-dynamical-systems frame) · `@bonnen2021` (binocular vision in foothold selection).

**Why we cite this corpus:** It is the **demonstrated record of success** NSF's criteria reward — the [[naturalistic-neuroscience|naturalistic]], multimodal, calibrated capture the instrument generalizes, and the PI's [[personnel|qualifications]].
