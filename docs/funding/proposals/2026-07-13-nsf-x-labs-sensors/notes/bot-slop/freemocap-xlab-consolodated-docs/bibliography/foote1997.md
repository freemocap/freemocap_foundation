# Foote & Yoder (1997) — Big Ball of Mud
**Citation:** Foote, B., & Yoder, J. (1997). *Big Ball of Mud.* Pattern Languages of Program Design 4 (PLoP). `@foote1997`

**What it is:** Names and analyzes the **Big Ball of Mud** — haphazardly structured, sprawling, duct-taped code — and observes it is the *de facto* most common architecture because the forces producing it (time pressure, no maintenance reward) are ubiquitous.

**Why we cite it:** The endpoint of unpaid [[cunningham1992|technical debt]] under [[lehman1980|Lehman's laws]] — the exact fate academic research software decays into ([[research-software-sustainability]], [[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]]).
