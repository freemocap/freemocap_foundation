# Quine (1951) — Two Dogmas of Empiricism
**Citation:** Quine, W. V. O. (1951). Two Dogmas of Empiricism. *The Philosophical Review* 60(1), 20–43. `@quine1951`

**What it is:** Confirmation holism: knowledge is an interconnected **web of belief** that "impinges on experience only along the edges"; we revise it to preserve coherence, and even core beliefs are revisable given enough contrary evidence.

**Why we cite it:** The **gradualist companion to [[kuhn1962|Kuhn]]**. The [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]] is a *shared, explicit, measurement-grounded web of belief* — every node ties to sensor evidence with [[uncertainty-budget|quantified uncertainty]], so when data conflicts, *which* beliefs to revise becomes legible rather than rhetorical. Invoke whichever lens (revolutionary/gradual) fits.
