# Barker et al. (2023) — Better Incentives Are Needed to Reward Academic Software Development
**Citation:** Barker, M., Katz, D. S., et al. (2023). Better Incentives Are Needed to Reward Academic Software Development. *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 7, 626–627. `@aac2023` · doi:10.1038/s41559-023-02008-w

**What it is:** States plainly that maintenance isn't rewarded, funding flows to new projects, and skilled research-software engineers leave for industry — so tools are maintained "only for a limited period," then decay.

**Why we cite it:** The consensus-of-a-subfield source that "academic software becomes abandonware" is **documented, not anecdotal** ([[research-software-sustainability]] C2) — the mechanism producing the thousand sloppy grad-student codebases, and the core of the "unmet by existing structures" argument.
