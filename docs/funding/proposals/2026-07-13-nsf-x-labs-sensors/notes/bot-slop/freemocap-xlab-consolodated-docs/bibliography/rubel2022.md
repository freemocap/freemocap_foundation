# Rübel et al. (2022) — The Neurodata Without Borders Ecosystem
**Citation:** Rübel, O., Tritt, A., Ly, R., et al. (2022). The Neurodata Without Borders Ecosystem for Neurophysiological Data Science. *eLife* 11, e78362. `@rubel2022` · doi:10.7554/eLife.78362

**What it is:** **NWB** — the neurophysiology archive standard and its ecosystem (surfaced through the DANDI archive).

**Why we cite it:** The neurophysiology half of the [[bids-and-nwb|BIDS/NWB]] archive layer — same dual role: a strong federated-standard [[tiered-dissemination-and-communal-science|dissemination]] precedent, but archival/post-hoc, not the operational capture instrument. BIDS lists NWB as only "unofficially-supported," illustrating the fragmentation the [[the-missing-middle|missing middle]] would unify.
