# Cunningham (1992) — The WyCash Portfolio Management System (technical debt)
**Citation:** Cunningham, W. (1992). The WyCash Portfolio Management System. *Addendum to OOPSLA '92.* `@cunningham1992` · doi:10.1145/157709.157715

**What it is:** Origin of the **technical debt** metaphor: shortcuts speed early development but must be "paid back promptly with a rewrite," or "the interest payments will grind the project to a halt."

**Why we cite it:** The mechanism behind the "Rome on Rome" pathology ([[path-dependence]] C3) — and why systems that never pay down debt become [[foote1997|Big Balls of Mud]]. Academia never funds the paydown.
