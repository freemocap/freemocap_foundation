# Akhshabi & Dovrolis (2011) — The Evolution of Layered Protocol Stacks Leads to an Hourglass Architecture
**Citation:** Akhshabi, S., & Dovrolis, C. (2011). *The Evolution of Layered Protocol Stacks Leads to an Hourglass-Shaped Architecture*. ACM SIGCOMM. `@akhshabi2011` · doi:10.1145/2018436.2018460

**What it is:** Formal analysis of *why* protocol stacks evolve toward a thin "waist" (few spanning-layer protocols, many above and below) — the hourglass/narrow-waist model that let the Internet scale.

**Why we cite it:** Evidence for the [[narrow-waist|narrow-waist]] design principle: a minimal, exact capture/timestamp representation is a thin waist you control, vs. a fat standard you import. Uses the program's own exemplar (the Internet, §7.0) as the reference class. Pair with [[beck2019]].
