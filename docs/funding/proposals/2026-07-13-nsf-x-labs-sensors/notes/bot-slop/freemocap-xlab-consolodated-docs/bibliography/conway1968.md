# Conway (1968) — How Do Committees Invent?
**Citation:** Conway, M. E. (1968). How Do Committees Invent? *Datamation* 14(4), 28–31. `@conway1968`

**What it is:** **Conway's Law**: "any organization that designs a system will inevitably produce a design that mirrors the organization's communication structure."

**Why we cite it:** The **linchpin** of the "why academia can't build this" argument ([[why-this-cant-be-academic-science]]): you can't get a refactorable, narrow-waisted instrument from a novelty-gated, maintenance-starved, publication-driven org — the architecture is downstream of the incentives. Fixing the instrument requires fixing the organization first, which is the literal reason the solicitation pairs "platform technology" with "new organizational structure." An X-Lab is the **Inverse Conway Maneuver**.
