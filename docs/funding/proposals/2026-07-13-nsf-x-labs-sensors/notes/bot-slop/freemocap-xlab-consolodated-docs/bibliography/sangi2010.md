# Sangi et al. (2010) — Torsion from Stable Iris Features
**Citation:** Sangi, M., et al. (2010). Measuring torsional eye movements by tracking stable iris features. *Journal of Neuroscience Methods*. `@sangi2010` · [PMC2958308](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2958308/) · [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165027010004449) · *(web-verified this session; **not yet in `FMCF-NSF-XLABS.bib`**)*

**What it is:** A video method for **ocular torsion** — tracks stable iris features (maximally-stable regions) about the pupil center, robust to nonuniform illumination and camera/eye position change; recovers horizontal, vertical, and torsional position.

**Why we cite it:** Candidate reference for the **torsion-from-iris-texture** claim ([[ocular-torsion-and-listings-law]], [[next-generation-eye-trackers]]) — the physics for measuring the "forgotten DOF" exists and needs better imaging to reach mobile use.
