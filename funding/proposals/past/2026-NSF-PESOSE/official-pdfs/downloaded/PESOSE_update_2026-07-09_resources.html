Important information for proposers and award recipients

All proposals must be submitted in accordance with the requirements specified in the funding opportunity and in the Proposal & Award Policies & Procedures Guide (PAPPG) and its supplements.

All NSF grants and cooperative agreements are subject to the applicable set of NSF award terms and conditions.

NSF has updated its research security policies for NSF funded projects.
