## Formatting requirements that will get you returned without review

- [ ] Title begins exactly `PESOSE: Track 1: <title>` — or `PESOSE / AI: Track 1: <title>` if responding to DCL 26-015. (SOL V.A / DCL)
- [ ] **Last line** of Project Summary is `Keywords:` followed by **2–5** keywords/phrases separated by semicolons, prioritized. (SOL V.A)
- [ ] **No URLs in the Project Description.** Pointer to the open-source product must be an in-line citation + References Cited entry. (SOL V.A)
- [ ] Project Description ≤ 7 pages. (SOL V.A)
- [ ] Budget ≤ $300,000, ≤ 1 year. Exceeding = may be returned without review. (WEB)

## Four things every PESOSE proposal must contain (SOL V.A)

- [ ] 1. Pointer to the existing publicly-available open-source product being transitioned (as citation, not URL)
- [ ] 2. Current status of the product: development and testing model, methods of dissemination, user base, contributor base
- [ ] 3. Description of the problem being addressed
- [ ] 4. Strong justification that the team is qualified to do this work

## Track 1 content areas (SOL V.A)

- [ ] **Ecosystem Discovery** — strategies to assess need for the innovation, justify *why an OSE is the right approach*, identify potential users and developers
- [ ] **Organization and Governance** — activities to establish governance + licensing models, development/integration processes, security and privacy safeguards, metrics for long-term success
- [ ] **Risk Analysis / Security Plan** — anticipated security, safety, privacy risks; mechanisms for quality assurance, secure modification and release, identity management, chain of custody
- [ ] **Community Building** — plans to engage users and developers (workshops, hackathons, competitions, research networks)

> **Track 1 awards support planning, not product development.** (SOL V.A, verbatim)

## Track 1 review criteria — the four questions reviewers score (SOL VI.A)

- [ ] 1. Convincing case the OSE addresses an issue of significant **societal or national importance not currently being adequately addressed**
- [ ] 2. Clear description of the **long-term vision for sustaining** the OSE
- [ ] 3. Clear description of a **recruitment strategy for new contributors** and for **growing the userbase**
- [ ] 4. **Specific, actionable list of milestones** and evaluation plan

Plus the two standard NSB criteria: Intellectual Merit, Broader Impacts.

NSF's own slide-deck emphasis: milestones "should cover all components, **beyond just the cyber technology**."

## Required Other Supplementary Documents

- [ ] **Letters of Collaboration — minimum 3, maximum 5.** (SOL V.A)
  - Must be from **current users or contributors not directly related to the proposing team**
  - Each must describe how they *have* contributed and *will continue* to contribute
  - ≤ 2 pages each; must state writer name, current affiliation, relationship to the team
  - Do **not** have to follow the standard PAPPG letter format
  - Plus: if the OSE will depend on facilities infrastructure from any org after the award ends, include one letter describing extent and term of that provision
- [ ] **List of Project Personnel, Collaborators, and Partner Organizations** (SOL V.A)
  - Tabular: Full name | Organization(s) | Role — in that order
  - Must include all PIs, co-PIs, Senior/Key Personnel, funded and unfunded consultants, collaborators (**everyone who wrote a letter of collaboration**), subawardees, postdocs, and project-level advisory committee members

## Budget mechanics — only on the website, not in the PDF (WEB)

- [ ] I-Corps for PESOSE line item, **up to $30,000**, is *mandatory* for Track 1
- [ ] Budget salary support for a team of **at least 3 and no more than 5** individuals for I-Corps
- [ ] Participation fee included (amount "posted on the PESOSE website" — **not located; needs an email to PESOSE@nsf.gov**)
- [ ] Nonprofit salary rates ≤ **75th-percentile BLS rate** for the role and geography
- [ ] For each employee: **SOC code + live link to the BLS page** in the budget justification
- [ ] NSF **does not recognize C-level titles** for salary-rate determination — map to responsibilities
- [ ] Salary calculations use **173.33 hours/month**
- [ ] Indirect: negotiated NICRA rate if one exists, else **15% de minimis** of modified total direct costs
- [ ] All Line A/B personnel are employees of the proposing organization
- [ ] **Owners/equity holders of the proposing entity may not be paid as consultants, contractors, or via subaward**
- [ ] No international travel on I-Corps funds; no gifts/meals for interviewees; no marketing/sales; no survey-tool purchases

## Eligibility gates to confirm before writing anything

- [ ] Active **UEI via SAM.gov** — registration "can take several weeks," and there are 28 days left
- [ ] Non-profit is U.S.-based **and >50% U.S.-owned and controlled**
- [ ] PI is an **employee of the proposing organization**, normally resident in the U.S.
- [ ] PI and all PESOSE-funded employees have legal right to work in the U.S.
- [ ] Single lead organization; any others are subawardees; ineligible orgs may not receive subawards

## Award obligations if funded (SOL V.A, WEB)

- I-Corps for PESOSE is **mandatory** for Track 1 awardees: kickoff, weekly sessions, closing meeting, and **≥100 stakeholder interviews**
- Roles to staff: Technical Lead/community manager, Entrepreneurial Lead/team lead, Industry Mentor
- EL and TL each spend ~1 day/week on discovery interviews
- Completing it as a Track 1 awardee **waives** the requirement for a future Track 2 award

## Explicit disqualifiers — NSF's own "What PESOSE Is (Not)" slide

- ❌ Not to fund **development** of open-source artifacts, tools, or products
- ❌ Not for **already well-resourced** open-source communities and ecosystems
- ❌ Not for open-source ventures where **profit is the primary motive**
