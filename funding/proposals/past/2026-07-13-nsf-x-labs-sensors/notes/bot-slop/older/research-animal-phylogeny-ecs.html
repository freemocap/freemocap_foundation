Composition, not inheritance

👁 The OpticalSensor slot

Compound eye (insects), camera eye (vertebrates), and a camera (artificial) all satisfy one component — transduce light (EAEE) — while sharing no mechanism. A shared interface, not a shared ancestor. This is what lets the same ontology reach sideways to robots & simulation.

One ecological trait, whole visual system

🎯 Predator vs Prey eyes

Prey (mouse, rat, guinea fowl): lateral eyes → panoramic field, little binocular overlap. Predator (ferret; and primates): frontal eyes → binocular overlap for depth. The Predator/Prey component propagates down through eye placement, cortical maps, and behavior.

Same task, different machinery

🧠 Optic tectum vs neocortex

Bird vision is routed through the optic tectum; mammalian vision through neocortex (tectum = superior colliculus in us). Guinea fowl vision is a functionalist probe — how is seeing solved with different hardware?

The sharpest point

🔀 Trait ≠ Lineage: ferret vs mouse

Phylogenetically, mice/rats sit on our branch (Euarchontoglires); the ferret is on the sister branch (Laurasiatheria) — farther from us. Functionally, the ferret's frontal eyes + orientation-column V1 are more human-like than a mouse's lateral eyes + salt-&-pepper V1. Convergence → a component ontology beats an inheritance tree. See it in the matrix: ferret shows green where mouse shows amber.

Physics hydration

🦴 Where rigid-body kinematics attaches

A RigidBody component (position, orientation, linear/angular momentum, inertia tensor) is valid only where segments are rigid — mineralized bone (Osteichthyes) or exoskeleton (arthropods). Soft-bodied agents (C. elegans) need continuum models.

camera→keypoints→segment→RigidBody

Momentum & angular momentum are derived objects carrying uncertainty propagated back to the primary optical sensor (metrology).

Grounding floor

🦠 Agency degree zero

Even a bacterium is an agent: chemoreceptors + flagellar motor = sense → act. That is where Empirically Available Environmental Energy (EAEE) first grounds the ontology — every richer nervous system is more components stacked on this loop.
