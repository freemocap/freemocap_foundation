# MoBI — Mobile Brain/Body Imaging

The named **peer-reviewed precedent** for the vision, coined by **Scott Makeig's** group (UCSD SCCN, ~2009): the *synchronous* collection of brain activity (EEG), behavior (motion capture, eye tracking), and environmental events, to study joint brain/body dynamics during natural cognition in 3D. Key names: Makeig, Klaus Gramann, Daniel Ferris.

It cuts two ways, both useful:

- **It validates the concept `#precedent`** — a ~15-year peer-reviewed lineage means a reviewer won't think the idea is crankish.
- **It exposes the gap `#reframe`** — MoBI is a **methodology, not a platform.** It is something heroic labs assemble by hand, per study, from incompatible parts; its toolboxes (MoBILAB, now unmaintained; the BeMoBIL pipeline) are batch post-processing scripts, not an operational data model. **The absence of the instrument is the bottleneck.**

> The pitch is not "invent multimodal capture" — it is "multimodal capture has never been turned into an actual instrument, and that absence throttles the field." See [[the-missing-middle]].
