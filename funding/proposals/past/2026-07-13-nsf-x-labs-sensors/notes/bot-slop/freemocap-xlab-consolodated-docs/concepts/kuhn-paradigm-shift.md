# Kuhn's Paradigm Shift (how you remake a field)

**Kuhn (1962):** paradigms shift not mainly by *persuading incumbents* but because a new paradigm **opens a field of solvable puzzles** that researchers gravitate toward. **Planck's principle** states it bluntly — science "advances one funeral at a time."

## The strategic corollary `#reframe`
The highest-leverage audience is the **youngest / most cross-disciplinary** layer of researchers. An instrument + grounded ontology + AI-navigability + a low-barrier [[boundary-objects|educational on-ramp]] is precisely a machine for **manufacturing "a more fruitful place" full of low-hanging fruit** — and for growing the generation native to it. *That* is how a platform **reshapes a field** (the exact Topic-2 Mission language), rather than competing within it. This is where the education thread stops being outreach and becomes **field-remaking strategy**.

**Complementary lens — Quine/Putnam's web of belief.** Where Kuhn is discontinuous, Quine's holism (*Two Dogmas*, 1951) is gradualist: knowledge is an interconnected fabric that "impinges on experience only along the edges." The [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]] is, in effect, a **shared, explicit, measurement-grounded web of belief** — every node ties to sensor evidence with [[uncertainty-budget|quantified uncertainty]], so when data conflicts, *which* beliefs to revise becomes legible rather than rhetorical. Invoke whichever fits.
