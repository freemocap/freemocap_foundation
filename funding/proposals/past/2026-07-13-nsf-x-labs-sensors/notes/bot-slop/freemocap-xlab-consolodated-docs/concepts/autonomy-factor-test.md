# The Autonomy Factor Test

The central eligibility bar for reaching **Phase 1**: NSF applies a factor test to assess a team's **independence**. A qualifying X-Lab must demonstrate:

- **Decisions in days, not weeks** — a leadership structure that acts independently of any parent institution.
- **Full internal control of funding** — no parent-institution approval to allocate/spend.
- **Full control of research direction & partnerships** — can shift technical approach without approval; may renegotiate milestones with NSF.
- **Full ownership and control of IP** — a parent institution must **not** own or control the X-Lab's IP.
- **Full control of hiring / salary / contracting.**
- **Independent leadership boards** — not shared with a parent.

Senior/Key Personnel and core leadership must be **full-time by the start of Phase 1**.
