# Maojo et al. (2011) — Biomedical Ontologies: Toward Scientific Debate
**Citation:** Maojo, V., Crespo, J., García-Remesal, M., et al. (2011). Biomedical Ontologies: Toward Scientific Debate. *Methods of Information in Medicine* 50(3), 203–216. `@maojo2011`

**What it is:** A sustained critique of **BFO's "stridently realist" metaphysics** and its practical cost for curators doing real terminology work.

**Why we cite it:** Evidence for the "[[obo-foundry|OBO is almost-perfect-but-clunky]]" claim — the philosophical baggage a general shared standard imposes. Supports the design decision to keep the *instrument's core* built-to-spec ([[build-to-spec-vs-shared-protocols]]) rather than inheriting BFO-realism overhead, while still using OBO as the model for the eventual shared layer.
