# Kanko et al. (2021) — Theia3D Validation vs. Marker-Based Capture
**Citation:** Kanko, R. M., Laende, E. K., Davis, E. M., Selbie, W. S., & Deluzio, K. J. (2021). Concurrent Assessment of Gait Kinematics Using Marker-Based and Markerless Motion Capture. *J. Biomechanics* 127, 110665. `@kanko2021` · doi:10.1016/j.jbiomech.2021.110665 · *(verified this session)*

**What it is:** Validates **Theia3D** (deep-learning markerless capture, 51 keypoints, trained on 500k+ annotated images) against Vicon across gait — comparable joint kinematics in all three planes.

**Why we cite it:** Names the **most direct commercial competitor** and takes it seriously ([[competitive-terrain]]). But Theia is **kinematics-only, single-modality** — the decisive read is that the edge is *integration, not capture accuracy*, where Theia (with the SIMM/OpenSim architect on its team) will win the pure-capture race. The crowded market becomes evidence *for* the integration-as-instrument thesis.
