# Zhang (2000) — A Flexible New Technique for Camera Calibration
**Citation:** Zhang, Z. (2000). A Flexible New Technique for Camera Calibration. *IEEE TPAMI* 22(11), 1330–1334. `@zhang2000` · doi:10.1109/34.888718

**What it is:** The standard camera-calibration method: recover intrinsics (focal length, principal point, distortion) + extrinsics by minimizing **reprojection error** (good calibrations < 1 px). The metrology of the very first pipeline link.

**Why we cite it:** Where `camera → keypoints → 3D kinematics` gets a real [[uncertainty-budget|uncertainty budget]] — with active work on characterizing the method's own uncertainty. Grounds the first, most concrete milestone in [[metrology-and-traceability]] and [[calibration]].
