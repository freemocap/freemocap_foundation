# Ashburner et al. (2000) — Gene Ontology
**Citation:** Ashburner, M., Ball, C. A., Blake, J. A., et al. (2000). Gene Ontology: Tool for the Unification of Biology. *Nature Genetics* 25(1), 25–29. `@ashburner2000` · doi:10.1038/75556

**What it is:** Founds the **Gene Ontology** — a controlled, structured vocabulary of `is_a`/`part_of`-linked terms describing gene function, built by the model-organism communities to stop everyone inventing incompatible vocabularies. One of the most-cited papers in biology.

**Why we cite it:** The origin of the [[obo-foundry|OBO/GO]] precedent — proof that a durable, community-owned scientific ontology is possible and enormously valuable, and that it took decades of sustained stewardship. Composition-over-inheritance applied to biology by biologists ([[composition-over-inheritance]]).
