# Brooks (1991) — Intelligence Without Representation
**Citation:** Brooks, R. A. (1991). Intelligence Without Representation. *Artificial Intelligence* 47(1–3), 139–159. `@brooks1991` · doi:10.1016/0004-3702(91)90053-M

**What it is:** Rodney Brooks's **Physical Grounding Hypothesis** for robotics: intelligence must be grounded in the physical world through sensors and action, not built atop abstract symbol manipulation — *"the world is its own best model."* (Note: this is **Rodney** Brooks; do not confuse with Frederick Brooks, [[brooks1975]].)

**Why we cite it:** The robotics-side twin of [[harnad1990|Harnad's]] symbol grounding, and the reason a measurement instrument (sensors + action in the world) is the right substrate. Reinforces that the [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]] is instrumentation. Also connects to the [[the-rw-to-ontology-pipeline|biological↔artificial loop]] (robot sense→plan→act mirrors the perceptual pipeline).
