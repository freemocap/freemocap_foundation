# Basic Formal Ontology (BFO) / ISO/IEC 21838-2
**Citation:** Smith, B., et al. (2020). *Basic Formal Ontology (BFO) 2.0 / ISO/IEC 21838-2.* `@bfo_iso` · github.com/bfo-ontology/bfo

**What it is:** The upper ontology mandated by the [[obo-foundry|OBO Foundry]], adopted in 350+ projects and standardized as ISO/IEC 21838-2 — the "do it right, governed" endpoint of a shared scientific standard.

**Why we cite it:** Shows both the **success** (a genuine cross-domain standard) and the **cost** (25 years, dozens of institutions, a resident philosopher, and the realism tax [[maojo2011]] documents) of building a shared protocol properly. Anchors the "you don't have that budget for the core" argument in [[build-to-spec-vs-shared-protocols]].
