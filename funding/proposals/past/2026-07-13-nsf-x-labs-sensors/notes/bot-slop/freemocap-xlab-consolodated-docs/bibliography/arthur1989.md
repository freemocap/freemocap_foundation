# Arthur (1989) — Competing Technologies, Increasing Returns, and Lock-In
**Citation:** Arthur, W. B. (1989). Competing Technologies, Increasing Returns, and Lock-In by Historical Events. *The Economic Journal* 99(394), 116–131. `@arthur1989` · doi:10.2307/2234208

**What it is:** Shows increasing returns + switching costs lock systems into **suboptimal equilibria that persist** even when better options exist.

**Why we cite it:** The economics companion to [[david1985|QWERTY]] for the [[path-dependence]] argument — why low-information early architecture choices become permanent ceilings, and why only a blank-page refactor ([[brooks1975]]) escapes them.
