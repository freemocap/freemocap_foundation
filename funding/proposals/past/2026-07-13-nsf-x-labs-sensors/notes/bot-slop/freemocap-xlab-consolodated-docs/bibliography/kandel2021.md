# Kandel et al. (2021) — Principles of Neural Science (6th ed.)
**Citation:** Kandel, E. R., Koester, J. D., Mack, S. H., & Siegelbaum, S. A. (2021). *Principles of Neural Science* (6th ed.). McGraw Hill. `@kandel2021`

**What it is:** The standard neuroscience reference; here specifically for **sensory transduction** — phototransduction (photoreceptors → membrane potential), mechanotransduction (hair cells → electrical signal in <1 ms).

**Why we cite it:** Textbook support for the deep parallel: nervous systems are, at their sensory surface, **arrays of [[sensor-as-transducer|transducers]] of environmental energy** — the same category of thing the instrument does. Makes the "studying perceptual-motor control *is* an instrumentation problem" argument rigorous rather than metaphorical.
