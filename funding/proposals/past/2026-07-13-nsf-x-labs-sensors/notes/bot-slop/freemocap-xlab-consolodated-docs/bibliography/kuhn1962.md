# Kuhn (1962) — The Structure of Scientific Revolutions
**Citation:** Kuhn, T. S. (1962). *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*. University of Chicago Press. `@kuhn1962`

**What it is:** Paradigms shift not mainly by persuading incumbents but because a new paradigm **opens a field of solvable puzzles** researchers gravitate toward (cf. Planck's principle, "science advances one funeral at a time").

**Why we cite it:** The **field-remaking strategy**: an instrument + grounded ontology + AI-navigability + low-barrier on-ramp is a machine for manufacturing "a more fruitful place" and growing the generation native to it — *how* a platform reshapes a field (Topic-2's Mission language) rather than competing within it.
