# Gorgolewski et al. (2016) — The Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS)
**Citation:** Gorgolewski, K. J., Auer, T., Calhoun, V. D., et al. (2016). The Brain Imaging Data Structure... *Scientific Data* 3, 160044. `@gorgolewski2016` · doi:10.1038/sdata.2016.44

**What it is:** BIDS — an **archival file-format convention** for organizing neuroimaging outputs, extended modality-by-modality (EEG, MEG, iEEG, motion, eye-tracking).

**Why we cite it:** Two faces. As a **[[tiered-dissemination-and-communal-science|dissemination]] precedent**, BIDS is the strongest model — a simple community standard that unlocked a field via federated archives. As a **positioning contrast**, it is *archival, fragmented, incompletely interoperable* — the [[bids-and-nwb|archive layer]] that presupposes but can't be the [[the-missing-middle|operational middle]]. Don't pitch another BIDS.
