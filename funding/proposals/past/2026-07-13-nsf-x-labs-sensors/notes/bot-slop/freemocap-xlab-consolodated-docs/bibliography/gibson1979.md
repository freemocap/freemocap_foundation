# Gibson (1979) — The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception
**Citation:** Gibson, J. J. (1979). *The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception*. Houghton Mifflin. `@gibson1979`

**What it is:** The founding statement of ecological optics. The environment provides perceptual information as **ambient energy arrays** (the ambient optic array), whose invariant structure directly specifies **affordances** — what surfaces are walk-on-able, graspable, sit-on-able. Perception is direct pickup of this structured energy, not inference over impoverished retinal images.

**Why we cite it:** This is the theoretical spine that ties the hardware to the science. [[eaee|EAEE]] is essentially Gibson's ambient array *named from the instrument's side of the transduction* — the instrument samples the same structured energy the animal's perceptual systems evolved to pick up. Grounds the claim that a [[sensor-grounded-ontology|sensor-grounded ontology]] is the *right* instrument for perceptual-motor science. Also the PI's own intellectual lineage (Gibson → Warren → Fajen; see [[personnel]]).
